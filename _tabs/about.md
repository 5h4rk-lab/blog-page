# About Me

Hey there! Welcome to my funky blog page. I'm absolutely stoked that you're here. Allow me to introduce myself.

## Who am I?

I am a quirky individual with a knack for all things tech and security-related. My real name is Go Charan Kilaru, but I prefer going by my sudo name, 5h4rk.

## What do I do?

As a tech enthusiast, I love diving into the exciting world of cybersecurity, constantly exploring and learning. I enjoy the thrill of solving challenges and puzzles, which is why I actively participate in Capture the Flag (CTF) competitions. It's like a wild adventure that puts my skills to the test.

In addition to my passion for CTFs, I am also a pentester. I dig deep into systems, searching for vulnerabilities and weaknesses, and then suggest solid defense measures to keep them secure. It's a unique way of making systems stronger and more resistant to attacks.

Apart from my security-focused activities, I have a wild side—I'm an adrenaline junkie! You can often find me shredding the waves as an avid surfer. It's a thrilling experience that helps me find balance amidst the ever-evolving tech world.
